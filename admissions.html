Admissions at Benhill

A thoughtful start to a bright future.

We understand that every child's educational journey is unique. Our admissions team can guide families through the next steps and explain the information needed for an application.

Please contact the school directly for the latest admissions policy, grade availability, fees and application deadlines.

Planning an application?

Before preparing documents, speak with the school to confirm the correct application procedure for the grade you are interested in. This page provides a general guide and is not a substitute for the college's official admissions instructions.

How to get started

The admission process.

Use these general steps as a starting point. The school will confirm the exact process and requirements for your application.

01

Make an enquiry

Contact the college to ask about the grade, entry requirements, available places and relevant dates.

02

Prepare your details

Ask the admissions team which application form and supporting documents are required before preparing them.

03

Follow school guidance

Submit the requested information using the school's official procedure and follow any further instructions provided.

Be prepared

Information to ask about.

Requirements can differ by grade and admission year. Confirm the current list with the college before submitting personal documents.

Ask an admissions question →

Check with the admissions office

  • Official application form and submission method
  • Birth certificate or other identity documentation, if required
  • Previous school reports or transfer documents, if applicable
  • Photographs or other supporting documents, if requested
  • Any interview, assessment or meeting requirements
  • Current fees, payment arrangements and important dates

This is a general checklist only, not a confirmed list of Benhill College's requirements. Please verify every item with the school.
